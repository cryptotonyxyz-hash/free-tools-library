How is PAYE tax calculated in Nigeria in 2026?

Under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, the first ₦800,000 of annual income is taxed at 0%, then 15% up to ₦3 million, 18% up to ₦12 million, 21% up to ₦25 million, 23% up to ₦50 million and 25% above that. Rent relief of 20% of your rent (capped at ₦500,000) reduces your taxable income, and anyone earning ₦840,000 a year or less pays no PAYE at all. The PAYE Tax Calculator works it out band by band for your salary.

How do I calculate the dividend I will earn on Nigerian (NGX) stocks?

Multiply the number of shares you own by the stock's dividend per share, then take off 10% withholding tax. The NGX Stock & Dividend Calculator does this for all 146 stocks on the Nigerian Exchange: enter how much you want to invest and it shows how many shares your money buys at the last closing price and the dividend you would receive after tax.

How do I calculate my liquidation price?

For an isolated-margin long, liquidation is roughly entry × (1 − 1/leverage + maintenance margin rate); for a short it is entry × (1 + 1/leverage − maintenance margin rate). The Liquidation Price Calculator does it from your entry, leverage and side, and shows how far price must move. Exchanges differ slightly, so treat it as an estimate.

How do I work out position size from my risk?

How do I calculate my BMI?

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres squared. Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is the normal WHO range, 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or above is obese. How many calories do I need each day?

Start with your BMR, the Mifflin-St Jeor equation uses your sex, age, weight and height, then multiply by an activity factor from 1.2 (sedentary) to 1.9 (very active) to get your TDEE, the calories that maintain your weight. The Calorie Calculator (TDEE) shows both, plus lose and gain targets.

What calorie deficit do I need to lose weight?

A deficit of about 500 kcal a day below your TDEE is the standard guide for losing roughly 0.5 kg a week; 250 kcal a day is a gradual pace and 1,000 kcal a day an intensive one. The Calorie Deficit Calculator works out your daily target at each pace and estimates the weeks to your target weight. Results are estimates, not medical advice, consult a clinician for personal health decisions.

Tracing works best on simple logos, icons and lettering, photos have too many tones and trace poorly. Craft users can download the SVG for Cricut and other cutting machines.

How do I convert markdown to PDF?

Paste or type your markdown into the Markdown to PDF Converter, check the live preview, then press Download PDF. Headings, lists, tables, quotes and code blocks are rendered into a clean A4 PDF, all in your browser. To edit markdown with an HTML export instead, use the Markdown Editor.

How do I calculate VAT in Nigeria?

Multiply the price by 1.075 to add VAT, or divide the total by 1.075 to remove it. The VAT Calculator Nigeria does both at the standard 7.5% rate under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Small businesses with turnover of ₦100 million or less are exempt from VAT registration.

How do I work out a discount price?

Multiply the original price by one minus the discount: 20% off ₦10,000 is ₦8,000, saving ₦2,000. The Discount Calculator does this, stacks a second discount, and works backwards from a sale price to the original price.

How do I calculate my profit margin?

Subtract the cost from the selling price, then divide by the selling price. A ₦60 cost sold at ₦100 is a 40% margin. The Profit Margin Calculator also shows markup, the price for a target margin, and break-even units and revenue.

How do I work out my freelance hourly rate?

Add your target monthly income to your monthly business costs, gross up for tax, then divide by your billable hours per year. The Freelance Rate Calculator does the sum and gives the minimum hourly rate and day rate.

How do I calculate my GPA in a Nigerian university?

Add your courses with their credit units and grades. On the 5.0 scale A is 5 points, B is 4, C is 3, D is 2, E is 1 and F is 0. Multiply each grade point by its units, add them up, and divide by your total units. The GPA and CGPA Calculator does it for you, shows your degree class, and works out what you need next semester to reach a target CGPA.

How do I calculate compound interest on my savings?

Enter your starting amount, annual interest rate, number of years and how often interest compounds, then add a monthly contribution if you save regularly. The Compound Interest Calculator shows your final balance, total interest earned and effective annual rate (APY), with a year-by-year breakdown.

How do I generate an APA or MLA citation for free?

Pick APA 7th or MLA 9th, choose your source type, book, journal article, website or newspaper article, and type the author, title, date and other details. The Citation Generator formats the reference and the in-text citation, and you can add entries to a bibliography and copy them all. Salary Calculator Nigeria, Take-Home Pay
What will you actually take home? Enter your gross monthly salary and this calculator takes off pension and NHIS, works out PAYE under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, and shows your real take-home pay, monthly and yearly.

  

    

      

      

    

    

      

      

    

    

    

    
Estimate only, not tax or payroll advice. By law, pension is 8% of your basic salary plus housing and transport allowances, and NHIS is 5% of basic salary, this calculator applies both percentages to your gross pay as a close estimate, because most people only know their gross. PAYE is then calculated on what is left after pension, NHIS and rent relief (20% of rent, capped at ₦500,000). Anyone earning ₦840,000 a year or less pays no PAYE. Other deductions, union dues, loans, cooperatives, are not included.

VAT Calculator Nigeria
Add 7.5% VAT to a price, or pull the VAT out of a price that already includes it. All figures in naira.

  

    
    

      
      
    

    

      

      

    

    

    
Estimates only, not tax advice. The standard VAT rate in Nigeria is 7.5% under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Small businesses with annual turnover of ₦100 million or less are exempt from VAT registration, so they do not charge VAT. VAT returns are filed with the Nigeria Revenue Service (formerly FIRS) by the 21st day of the month after the transaction. Confirm anything that affects a filing with an accountant or the NRS.

Profit Margin Calculator
Cost and selling price in, margin, markup and profit per unit out. Add your monthly fixed costs to see how many units you must sell to break even.

  

    

      

      

    

    

      

      

    

    
Break-even (optional)

    

      

      

    

    

    
Margin is profit as a share of the selling price; markup is profit as a share of the cost. A 50% markup is only a 33.3% margin, so do not mix them up when you price.

    
This is gross margin: it covers direct unit cost only. Rent, salaries, marketing and tax are not deducted, so it is not a net profit figure. Estimates only, not financial advice.

Compound Interest Calculator - Grow Your Savings
See how your savings grow when interest earns interest. Enter a starting amount, a rate and a number of years, add a monthly contribution if you save regularly, and get your final balance, total interest and effective annual rate.

  

    

      

      

    

    

      

      

    

    

      

      

    

    

    
Formula: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is your starting amount, r is the annual rate, n is how many times interest compounds per year, and t is the number of years. Monthly contributions are added at the end of each month.

    
Estimates only, not financial advice. A fixed rate is assumed, but real investments vary and returns are not guaranteed. Taxes, fees and inflation are not included. Confirm actual rates with your bank or investment provider.
