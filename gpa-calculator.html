About this GPA and CGPA calculator

Most Nigerian universities grade on a 5.0 scale, so this calculator puts 5.0 first: A is 5 points, B is 4, C is 3, D is 2, E is 1 and F is 0. Universities like LASU, UNILAG and OAU use this system, though grade bands can differ slightly between schools, so always confirm yours with your department. A 4.0 scale option is included for US and international courses. Your GPA is the average for one semester, your CGPA is the average across all your semesters, and the calculator shows both, plus your degree class on the 5.0 scale.

How to use the GPA calculator

  1. Pick your scale - 5.0 for Nigerian universities, 4.0 for US and international courses.
  2. Add your courses - course code, credit units and grade for each course this semester. The GPA updates as you type.
  3. Save the semester - press Save this semester to add it to your running total, then enter the next semester to build your CGPA.
  4. Plan your target - type your current CGPA, your units so far, your target CGPA and next semester's units to see the average grade you need.

What you get

  • Semester GPA - grade points and units for the courses you entered, on the 5.0 or 4.0 scale.
  • Cumulative CGPA - save each semester and watch your CGPA build across all of them.
  • Degree classification - First Class, Second Class Upper and Lower, Third Class or Pass, on the 5.0 scale.
  • Target planner - the average grade you need next semester to reach the CGPA you are aiming for.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my GPA in a Nigerian university?
Add your courses with their credit units and grades. On the 5.0 scale A is 5 points, B is 4, C is 3, D is 2, E is 1 and F is 0. Multiply each grade point by its units, add them up, and divide by your total units. The GPA and CGPA Calculator does it for you, shows your degree class, and works out what you need next semester to reach a target CGPA.
What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?
GPA is your grade point average for one semester only, while CGPA is the cumulative average across all your semesters so far. To get your CGPA, save each semester in the calculator and it adds every semester's points and units together.
What CGPA do I need for a First Class in Nigeria?
On the 5.0 scale used by most Nigerian universities, First Class is 4.50 to 5.00, Second Class Upper is 3.50 to 4.49, Second Class Lower is 2.40 to 3.49, Third Class is 1.50 to 2.39 and Pass is 1.00 to 1.49. Confirm your own university's bands with your department, because they can differ slightly.
