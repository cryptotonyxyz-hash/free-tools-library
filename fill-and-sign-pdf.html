Fill & Sign PDF - Add Text, Dates & a Signature to Any PDF

A form that is “just a PDF”, no fillable fields, nothing to click? Upload it here anyway. Type your answers, add the date, draw your signature, drag everything into place on the rendered pages, and download a properly filled PDF.

About this tool

Plenty of official PDFs are flat: they look like forms but have no form fields at all, so the usual advice is to print, write by hand, scan and send, a whole afternoon gone. This tool takes the direct route instead. Your PDF's pages are rendered in your browser, and you place items on top of them: text for names and answers, a date, and a real handwritten signature you draw with your mouse or finger on the signature pad.

Every item can be dragged anywhere on its page and resized with the green corner handle, so a signature can sit exactly on the signature line and a name exactly in its box. When it looks right, pdf-lib writes the items into a brand-new PDF and you download it. It works on any PDF, even without form fields, because nothing here depends on the file having fields, and your document is never uploaded anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Upload the PDF, its pages are rendered in your browser, ready to work on.
  2. Add your items, type text and pick its size, pick a date, or draw your signature on the pad; for each one, choose which page it belongs on.
  3. Position everything, drag each item where it belongs on the page, and drag the green corner dot to make it bigger or smaller. The ✕ on an item removes it.
  4. Download the filled PDF, press “Download filled PDF” and save the finished file, filled.pdf.

What you get

  • Works without form fields, items are placed on top of the rendered page, so any PDF can be filled, fielded or not.
  • Real handwritten signature, drawn on a signature pad with your mouse or finger, in pen-blue ink.
  • Move and resize freely, drag items to the exact line or box; resize with the corner handle.
  • Any page, any number of items, text, dates and signatures across the whole document.
  • No uploads, rendering and filling happen on your device with pdf.js and pdf-lib.

Frequently asked questions

How do I fill and sign a PDF that has no form fields?
Upload the PDF above, then add text, a date or a signature and pick its page. Drag each item into position on the rendered page, resize it with the green corner handle, and download the filled PDF. It works on any PDF, even without fillable form fields, because the items are placed on top of the page.
How do I sign the PDF?
Draw your signature on the signature pad with your mouse or finger, press “Add signature to page”, then drag it onto the signature line and resize it to fit. Clear the pad and redraw as many times as you need before adding it.
Does filling change my original PDF?
No. The tool builds a new PDF with your items written in and downloads that as filled.pdf. Your original file is untouched, and it never leaves your device.
Is this a legal electronic signature service?
This tool places your handwritten signature image and typed details onto the document, which is what most everyday forms need. It is not a certified digital-signature or audit-trail service, if a document legally requires a certified e-signature, use the service the sender specifies.
