How to Use the BMR Calculator, Step by Step, and What It Is Good For

The calories your body burns doing nothing at all, worked out with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation from your sex, age, weight and height.

Your basal metabolic rate is the energy your body spends just staying alive, breathing, circulating blood, keeping warm, measured at complete rest. BMR is a foundation, not a target: nobody eats at BMR, because nobody lives at rest, multiply it by activity to get your TDEE in the Calorie Calculator.
