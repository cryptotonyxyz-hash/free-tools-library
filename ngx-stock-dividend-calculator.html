About this NGX stock and dividend calculator

The calculator covers all 146 NGX-listed stocks at their last close (9 November 2026). Dividend per share is pre-filled for the stocks with verified full-year 2025 figures; where a figure is not verified, the calculator says so plainly and lets you type in the dividend per share yourself rather than guessing. Your result shows the number of shares your money buys, the yearly dividend before tax, the 10% withholding tax, and what actually lands in your account. Figures are estimates for planning, not investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the dividend I will earn on Nigerian (NGX) stocks?
Multiply the number of shares you own by the stock's dividend per share, then take off 10% withholding tax. This calculator does it for all 146 NGX stocks: enter how much you want to invest and it shows the shares your money buys at the last closing price and the dividend after tax.
Which NGX stock prices does the calculator use?
The last closing price for each of the 146 stocks on the Nigerian Exchange, as at 9 November 2026. Prices are a snapshot for planning, not a live quote.
How much tax is taken off Nigerian stock dividends?
Dividends in Nigeria carry a 10% withholding tax, which the company deducts before paying you. The calculator deducts it automatically and shows your net dividend.
