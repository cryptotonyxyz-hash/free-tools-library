About this VAT calculator

Value Added Tax in Nigeria is charged at a standard rate of 7.5% on taxable goods and services, under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. This calculator does the two sums people actually need: add VAT to a price that excludes it, and remove VAT from a price that already includes it. The second one is where most people go wrong: you cannot just take 7.5% off a VAT-inclusive price, because the 7.5% was added to the smaller number. The calculator divides by 1.075 instead, which is the correct way. Small businesses with annual turnover of ₦100 million or less are exempt from VAT registration, so they do not charge VAT. Figures are estimates for guidance, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in Nigeria?
The standard VAT rate in Nigeria is 7.5%, under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. It applies to taxable supplies of goods and services. Some items, like unprocessed basic food, medical services, education and books, are exempt or zero-rated.
How do I remove VAT from a price that includes it?
Divide the total by 1.075. For example, ₦10,750 including VAT is ₦10,000 before VAT plus ₦750 VAT. Taking 7.5% off ₦10,750 gives ₦806, which is wrong, because the 7.5% was added to the smaller number. The VAT Calculator Nigeria does this division for you.
Do small businesses charge VAT in Nigeria?
Businesses with annual turnover of ₦100 million or less are exempt from VAT registration under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, so they do not charge VAT. Above that threshold, a business must register and charge VAT on taxable supplies.
