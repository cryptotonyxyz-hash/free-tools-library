About this tool

A good caption does three jobs: it stops the scroll, it sounds like you, and it gives the post its hashtags without a separate research session. This generator writes five options at once from two inputs, your topic or mood, and a tone: Funny (playful, a little chaotic), Inspirational (uplifting and motivational), Aesthetic (soft, minimal, film-photo vibes), Promotional (written for selling, with DM-to-order calls to action) or Casual (everyday and low-key).

Every caption arrives finished: emojis placed where a human would put them, and a hashtag block built from your topic plus the tone’s tags. Each caption has its own copy button, and the Shuffle button rotates the templates and tags for a fresh set whenever the first five do not fit. The captions are generated on your device from your inputs, nothing is uploaded, and there is no sign-up. Tweak a word or two before posting so it sounds like you, not like a template.

How to use it

  1. Enter your topic or mood, “beach day in Lagos”, “my new haircut”, “launching my shoe store”, anything.
  2. Pick a tone, Funny, Inspirational, Aesthetic, Promotional or Casual.
  3. Browse the five captions, each one complete with emojis and a hashtag block.
  4. Copy the one you like, or press Shuffle for a fresh set, then paste it into Instagram and make it yours.

Frequently asked questions

How many hashtags should an Instagram caption have?
Instagram allows up to 30, but for most posts 5 to 10 relevant hashtags is the sweet spot, enough to place the post in the right searches and feeds without looking spammy. Every caption from this generator ships with 6: your topic tag, four tone tags and #instagood.
Which tone should I pick?
Match the tone to the post’s job. Selling something? Promotional, with its order-by-DM calls to action. A personal photo with friends? Casual or Funny. A moody, curated shot? Aesthetic. A milestone or a Monday-morning post? Inspirational. When in doubt, generate two tones and compare.
Can I edit a caption before posting?
Please do, the captions are strong starting points, and one personal detail (a name, a place, an inside joke) makes them unmistakably yours. Copy the caption, paste it into Instagram, and adjust the wording there before you share.
