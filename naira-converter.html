About this naira converter

Most currency converters show the naira rate as a long decimal that nobody in Nigeria recognises. This naira converter flips it: the rate is quoted as naira per one dollar, pound or euro, the way bureau de change operators, traders and freelancers actually talk about it. It converts between NGN, USD, GBP and EUR. The rate is a snapshot loaded when the page was built, so check the current rate before a big transaction.

How to use the naira converter

  1. Pick your currency, naira, dollars, pounds or euros.
  2. Type an amount, enter how much you want to convert.
  3. Read the results, the amount in every other currency, plus the rate quoted as ₦ per $1.
  4. Convert both ways, naira to foreign currency works exactly the same way.

What you get

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert dollars to naira?
Enter the dollar amount in the converter above and it shows the naira value, with the rate quoted the way people say it in Nigeria, like ₦1,350 to $1, instead of a decimal.
Why does the converter show the rate as ₦ per $1?
Because that is how exchange rates are quoted in everyday Nigerian use. A rate like 0.00074 means nothing to most people; ₦1,350 to $1 is instantly clear.
Which currencies can I convert?
US dollars (USD), British pounds (GBP), euros (EUR) and Nigerian naira (NGN), in both directions.
