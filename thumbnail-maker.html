Thumbnail Maker – Bold Thumbnails for X, YouTube & Instagram

Thumbnails are headlines with a photo attached. Upload your image, type the bold words, pick the platform size — X, YouTube or Instagram — and download a finished thumbnail without opening a design app.

About this tool

A working thumbnail needs three things: the right dimensions for the platform, a photo that fills the frame, and a few big words a viewer can read at a glance on a phone. This maker handles all three in your browser: your photo is cover-cropped to 1600×900 for X, 1280×720 for YouTube, or 1080×1080 / 1080×1350 for Instagram; a dark gradient keeps the text readable; and the headline is drawn in a heavy display font with a thick outline, auto-shrinking to fit up to three lines, with an accent bar and an optional smaller second line. No upload, no watermark, no design account — the PNG downloads straight from the canvas on your device.

How to use it

  1. Upload your image — it is cropped to fill the size you pick; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  2. Choose the platform size — X, YouTube, Instagram square or Instagram portrait.
  3. Type the headline — short and loud wins; adjust its size, colour and position.
  4. Download the PNG — full resolution, ready to upload with your post or video.

What you get

  • Platform sizes built in — 1600×900, 1280×720, 1080×1080 and 1080×1350.
  • Readable by design — gradient scrim, heavy outline and auto-fit headline up to three lines.
  • Colour & position controls — white, yellow, green or red text, top, centre or bottom.
  • Fully private — the image is drawn on a canvas in your browser and never leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?
1280 × 720 pixels is YouTube's recommended thumbnail size (16:9). For X, 1600 × 900 displays cleanly on posts; Instagram feed posts are 1080 × 1080 square or 1080 × 1350 portrait. All four are presets in the maker.
How many words should a thumbnail headline have?
Three to five big words is the sweet spot — they must be readable at the tiny size thumbnails appear in feeds. The maker has a smaller second line for one short supporting phrase.
Will my photo be cropped?
Yes — to fill the frame (cover-crop), the sides or top and bottom are trimmed depending on your photo's shape, exactly like design tools do. A photo already close to the target ratio crops least.
Is there a watermark?
No. The PNG you download is exactly what you see in the preview.
