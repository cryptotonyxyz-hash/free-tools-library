About this tool

Designers and developers juggle colour formats all day: a designer hands over a HEX, a stylesheet wants HSL, a shadow needs RGBA with opacity. This picker keeps every format in sync, move any slider (red, green, blue, hue, saturation, lightness or opacity) and all six formats update together. The preview sits on a transparency checkerboard so opacity changes are honest, the random button is good for placeholder palettes, and the recent-picks row holds the colours you settled on while you work, so you can jump back without writing anything down.

How to use it

  1. Set your colour, use the visual picker, type a HEX value, or drag the RGB and HSL sliders.
  2. Adjust opacity, the slider from 100% down to fully transparent, previewed on a checkerboard.
  3. Copy the format you need, HEX, HEX8, RGB, RGBA, HSL or HSLA, each with its own copy button.
  4. Keep or shuffle, settled colours land in your recent picks; hit Random colour when you want a fresh idea.

What you get

  • Six formats, always in sync, HEX, HEX8 (with alpha), RGB, RGBA, HSL and HSLA.
  • RGB, HSL and opacity sliders, plus a visual picker and direct HEX typing, including 3-digit and 8-digit HEX.
  • Random colours, generated with secure randomness for palettes and placeholders.
  • Recent-picks history, tap any swatch to bring a colour back; it lives only in your tab.

Frequently asked questions

What is HEX8 and when do I need it?
HEX8 is a normal 6-digit HEX colour plus two more digits for opacity (alpha), for example #0B6B3A80 is the library green at 50% opacity. It works in CSS anywhere a colour does, and the picker shows it alongside the RGBA equivalent.
How is opacity shown in RGB and HSL?
With an alpha value between 0 and 1: rgba(11, 107, 58, 0.5) is the same colour at half opacity. The picker converts for you, so you never have to do the maths.
Can I type a colour instead of using the sliders?
Yes, type any 3-digit, 6-digit or 8-digit HEX value (with or without the #) and the sliders and formats all update.
Are my recent picks saved anywhere?
No. The history lives in the page while the tab is open and disappears when you leave, nothing is uploaded or stored.
