About this salary calculator

The PAYE calculator answers “how much tax do I owe?” — this one answers the question people actually ask at offer time: “what lands in my account?” Pension (8%) and NHIS (5%) come off your gross pay first, rent relief (20% of your rent, capped at ₦500,000) reduces what is taxable, and PAYE is then worked out band by band under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025: 0% on the first ₦800,000 a year, then 15% up to ₦3 million, 18% to ₦12 million, 21% to ₦25 million, 23% to ₦50 million and 25% above that. Earn ₦840,000 a year or less and your PAYE is ₦0. Two honest simplifications: by law, pension is 8% of basic plus housing and transport, and NHIS is 5% of basic — this calculator applies both to gross pay, because most people only know their gross figure. Figures are estimates for guidance, not payroll or tax advice.

How to use the salary calculator

  1. Enter your gross monthly salary — the full amount before any deductions.
  2. Add your annual rent (optional) — it earns rent relief of 20%, capped at ₦500,000, off your taxable income.
  3. Choose your deductions — pension (8%) and NHIS (5%) are on by default; switch either off if it does not apply to you.
  4. Read your take-home — monthly and annual net pay, each deduction, and the band-by-band PAYE breakdown.

What you get

Frequently asked questions

How is take-home pay calculated in Nigeria?
Start with gross pay. Take off pension (8%) and NHIS (5%), then apply rent relief (20% of rent, capped at ₦500,000) to get taxable income. PAYE is charged band by band under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 — 0% to ₦800,000, 15% to ₦3m, 18% to ₦12m, 21% to ₦25m, 23% to ₦50m and 25% above — and what remains is your take-home pay.
What is the difference between this and the PAYE Tax Calculator?
The PAYE Tax Calculator works out the tax on its own, with a band breakdown. This salary calculator answers the whole question — gross pay in, PAYE plus pension plus NHIS out — and shows the net salary you actually receive.
Why does my payslip show slightly different figures?
By law, pension is 8% of basic salary plus housing and transport allowances (not always the full gross), and NHIS is 5% of basic salary. This calculator applies both percentages to your gross pay as a close estimate, and it does not include union dues, cooperative or loan deductions, so treat the result as a guide.
Who pays no tax on their salary in Nigeria?
Anyone earning ₦840,000 a year or less (₦70,000 a month) pays no PAYE at all under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Pension and NHIS may still be deducted if your employer operates those schemes.
