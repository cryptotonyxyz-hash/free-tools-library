About this invoice generator

This free invoice maker is built for freelancers, traders and small businesses in Nigeria and beyond who need a proper invoice without paying for accounting software or wrestling with a spreadsheet template. The invoice number starts at INV0001 and counts up, prices are in naira (₦) by default with a dollar ($) option, and you can set the invoice as due on receipt or pick a due date. What you see in the live preview is exactly what downloads, no watermark, no sign-up, and your details never leave your device.

How to use the invoice generator

  1. Add your business details, your business name, contact details and an optional logo.
  2. Add your client, the name and details of who the invoice is for, plus the invoice date and due date.
  3. List your items, each product or service with its quantity and price; totals update live.
  4. Download, save the finished invoice as a print-ready A4 PDF or a PNG image and send it to your client.

What you get

  • Live totals, subtotal and grand total update as you type, in ₦ or $.
  • Your branding, upload your logo and it appears on the invoice.
  • Bank details built in, add your account details so clients can pay straight away.
  • PDF and PNG downloads, a clean A4 PDF for email, or an image for WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an invoice online for free?
Add your business name and logo, your client's details, and each item with its quantity and price in the generator above. The total updates as you type, and you can download the finished invoice as a PDF or PNG image, no sign-up and no watermark.
Can I put my own logo and bank details on the invoice?
Yes. Upload your business logo and type your bank name, account number and account name into the payment details section, both appear on the downloaded invoice so your client can pay you directly.
What currency does the invoice generator use?
Naira (₦) is the default, with a dollar ($) option. The invoice number starts at INV0001, and you can set payment as due on receipt or choose a specific due date.
