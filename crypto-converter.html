About this tool

The converter pulls live prices for eleven major coins, BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE, TRX, LTC, USDT and USDC, from CoinGecko's free public API, which needs no key. Each result shows the price of one coin, your amount in both NGN and USD, and the implied naira-to-dollar rate behind the conversion, so you can sanity-check it against the rate you would actually get. You can also edit the price box to convert at your own rate, useful for P2P quotes. Prices are mid-market guides: the rate on an exchange or P2P desk in Nigeria will differ a little, and the tool says so on the page.

How to use it

  1. Pick the coin, eleven majors, from Bitcoin to USDC.
  2. Type the amount, a whole coin, a fraction, whatever you hold.
  3. Choose naira or dollars, the other currency is shown too, for reference.
  4. Check the stamp, every result names CoinGecko as the source and shows when the price was fetched.

What you get

  • Live CoinGecko prices, fetched fresh when you open the tool, refreshable any time.
  • NGN first, naira values shown the way Nigerians quote them, with the implied ₦/$ rate.
  • Editable rate, type your own price to convert at a P2P or exchange quote.
  • 11 coins, BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE, TRX, LTC, USDT and USDC.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the prices come from?
CoinGecko's free public price API, fetched live in your browser when the tool opens. The result shows the fetch time. If the API is rate-limited, the tool tells you and lets you type a rate manually.
Why is the naira value different from what an exchange offers me?
This is a mid-market global price converted at the implied rate. Nigerian exchanges and P2P desks add their own spread, so your sell price will usually be a little lower. Treat this as the reference value, not a quote.
Can I convert dollars or naira back into crypto?
Yes, in effect: the result shows the price of one coin, so dividing your naira or dollar amount by that price gives the coin amount. The implied ₦/$ rate on the result card helps you cross-check.
Which coins are supported?
Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), BNB, Solana (SOL), XRP, Cardano (ADA), Dogecoin (DOGE), TRON (TRX), Litecoin (LTC), Tether (USDT) and USD Coin (USDC).
