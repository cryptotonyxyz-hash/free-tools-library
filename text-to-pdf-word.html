About this text to PDF and Word converter

Sometimes you just need a document: a letter for an application, a statement for a bank, notes tidied into a file. This text to PDF and Word converter keeps it simple, add a title, type or paste your text, and download a properly formatted PDF for sending, or a Word document when the other side needs to edit it. It runs in your browser with no sign-up, so drafts and personal letters stay on your device.

How to use the text to PDF and Word converter

  1. Add a title, the heading that appears at the top of your document.
  2. Type or paste your text, your letter, notes or statement.
  3. Choose your format, PDF for a finished document, Word for an editable one.
  4. Download, save the file and send or print it.

What you get

  • PDF and Word, a finished PDF, or an editable Word document, from the same text.
  • Clean formatting, a titled, readable document, not a raw text dump.
  • Fast for simple documents, letters, statements and notes in under a minute.
  • Private, the document is created in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn text into a PDF for free?
Type or paste your text into the converter above, add a title, choose PDF and download. The document is created in your browser, no sign-up and no upload.
Can I download my text as a Word document instead?
Yes. Choose the Word option and you get an editable document you can open and change in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or similar apps.
What is this converter best for?
Short, simple documents: application letters, personal statements, notes, minutes and drafts, anything where you need a clean file quickly without a full word processor.
