Frequently asked questions

How do I convert PNG to JPG?
Add your PNG image to the converter above, choose JPG as the output format, and download. The conversion happens in your browser in seconds.
How do I convert a WebP image to PNG or JPG?
Choose the WebP file, pick PNG or JPG as the target format, and convert. This is the fix when a website or app refuses to accept a WebP upload.
Does converting an image reduce its quality?
Converting to PNG keeps the image as-is. JPG and WebP are smaller, lossy formats, so there is a small quality trade-off in exchange for a much smaller file.
