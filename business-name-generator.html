About this business name generator

A good business name is short, easy to say and easy to remember. This generator builds name ideas from your keyword, your category and the style you pick: modern names for a fresh feel, classic names for trust, playful names for a friendly brand, premium names for a high-end feel, or short and catchy names that stick. The Nigeria flavour mixes in Nigerian words and names like Naija, Eko, Ada and Zuri, so the name can sound local as well as professional. Every name comes with its own copy button, and the shuffle button gives you a fresh set in one tap. Before you commit to a name, check that it is not already taken, and check domain and CAC availability.

How to use the business name generator

  1. Describe your business - type a keyword or topic, like cakes, fashion or tech repair. Leave it blank to generate from the category alone.
  2. Pick a category - fashion, food, tech, beauty, fitness, consulting, shop, creative, real estate, agriculture or general.
  3. Choose a style and the Nigeria flavour - modern, classic, playful, premium or short and catchy, with the Nigeria flavour on or off.
  4. Copy the name you like - tap Copy on any name, or Shuffle for a fresh set of twelve.

What you get

  • Twelve ideas per set - a fresh batch every time you generate or shuffle.
  • Nigeria flavour - Nigerian words and names mixed in, or plain English names only.
  • Five styles - modern, classic, playful, premium and short and catchy.
  • One-tap copy - copy any name straight to your clipboard.

Frequently asked questions

How do I come up with a good business name?
Start with what the business does, in one or two words, and type that into the Business Name Generator. Pick the category that fits and a style that matches the feeling you want, modern, classic, playful, premium or short. The generator gives you twelve ideas: copy the one that is short, easy to say and easy to remember.
What does the Nigeria flavour do?
It mixes Nigerian words and names into the suggestions, like Naija, Eko, Ada, Chidi, Amara and Zuri, so the name can sound local as well as professional. Switch it off for plain English names only.
Is a name from this generator free to use?
The ideas are free suggestions, but a name can still be taken. Before you commit, check that no other business is using it, check the domain is available, and check CAC registration if you plan to register the business.
