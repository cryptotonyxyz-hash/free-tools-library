About this citation generator

A citation generator formats the details of your source into the reference list entry your style guide wants, plus the short in-text citation that points to it. This one covers APA 7th edition and MLA 9th edition, the two styles most Nigerian and international universities ask for, and four source types: books, journal articles, websites and newspaper articles. You type the details, it formats them. Nothing is looked up online, so no DOI or URL is ever sent anywhere.

How to use the citation generator

  1. Pick a style - APA 7th edition or MLA 9th edition.
  2. Pick a source type - book, journal article, website or newspaper article. The form shows only the fields that style and type need.
  3. Type the details - author, year, title, and the container, publisher, volume, issue, pages or DOI where they apply. Separate multiple authors with a semicolon.
  4. Copy or save it - copy the reference or the in-text citation, or add it to your bibliography and copy the whole list at the end.

Frequently asked questions

How do I generate an APA citation for free?
Pick APA 7th edition, choose your source type, book, journal article, website or newspaper article, and type the author, title, date and other details. The Citation Generator formats the reference and the in-text citation, and you can add entries to a bibliography and copy them all.
What is the difference between APA and MLA citations?
APA 7th puts the year right after the author, uses initials for first names, and uses sentence case for titles, so only the first word and proper nouns are capitalised. MLA 9th puts the year near the end, spells out first names, and keeps titles as published. APA in-text citations use author and year, MLA uses author and page number.
Do I need to check the citation before I submit it?
Yes. This tool formats the details you type, but style guides have edge cases it cannot know about, like corporate authors, missing dates, or unusual source types. Always check the finished entry against the official APA or MLA style guide before you submit your paper.
